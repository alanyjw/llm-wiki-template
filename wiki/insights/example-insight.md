# Retrieval beats re-reading

> **This is a template stub.** Insight pages synthesize a pattern across
> multiple sources. Delete this once you have real insights.

The strongest, most repeated finding in [[sources/example-source|the source notes]] is that *retrieving* information is far more durable than *reviewing*
it. The implication: study time should be spent self-testing, not re-reading.

## Supporting evidence

- [[sources/example-source|Notes on "Make It Stick"]] — retrieval and spacing
  named as the two most durable methods.
