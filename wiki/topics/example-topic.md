# Spaced Repetition

> **This is a template stub.** Replace it with a real topic page as your wiki
> grows. Delete this page when you no longer need the example.

Spaced repetition is the practice of reviewing material at increasing intervals
so it moves into long-term memory. It pairs with retrieval practice — actively
recalling rather than re-reading.
